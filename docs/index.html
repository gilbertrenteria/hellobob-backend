What I Do

One Front Desk. Every Channel. Zero Missed Jobs.

I'm Bob, your real virtual front desk, trained on your business — your services, your service area, how you talk to customers. I sit wherever a customer might reach out, have the conversation, and hand you a booked job. Here's the whole flow:

01

I Answer Instantly

The moment someone opens a chat from your website, taps through from Facebook, Instagram, or your Google Business Profile, or texts your business line, I reply in seconds — nights, weekends, holidays. No hold music. No voicemail. No "we'll call you back."

02

I Qualify The Job

I ask what a good dispatcher would ask: what's broken, how urgent, what kind of unit, what address. By the time I loop you in, the job is already scoped.

03

I Book The Job

Once I have what I need, I put the appointment straight onto your calendar and send you the summary. You wake up to booked work, not a pile of messages to sort through.

04

I Work From Anywhere

No app, nothing to log into. I run as your website chat — reachable from a Facebook or Instagram bio link, your Google Business Profile's Website button, a missed-call text-back, or nothing more than a simple link or a QR code on a truck, invoice, or yard sign.

05

I Keep The Job On Track

Once it's booked, I keep the follow-through automatic: a reminder before the appointment, a heads-up when the tech's on the way, a booking confirmation and payment-reminder email, and later, a nudge to get back on the schedule for the next tune-up.

06

I Follow Up For Reviews

Once the job's done, I text (and email, if you've turned that on) the customer to rate the visit. 4–5 stars go straight to Google or Facebook — anything lower comes to you privately, before it's ever posted.

Common Questions

Before you ask.

Will you actually sound like my business?

Yes. I'm configured with your real hours, pricing, and services before I ever talk to a customer — not a generic script shared across every company using me.

What happens if you can't answer something?

I capture the customer's name and number and hand the conversation to you directly, instead of letting them leave with no way to follow up.

Do I need to know anything technical to set you up?

No. You send us your business details, we configure everything on our end, and you paste one short snippet into your website — that's the whole setup.

How do I access my dashboard?

We email you a login link for app.hellobob.com right after you sign up, sent to the address you gave us during setup — from there you just log in whenever you want to check leads, bookings, or reviews.

What if my team already answers my phones?

I cover a different channel entirely — the visitors who'd rather type a question on your website than make a call. I run alongside whatever phone setup you already have; nothing about your phones needs to change.

Do you charge more if I run more trucks or a bigger crew?

No. It's the same $99/month whether you're a one-truck outfit or running a full fleet — pricing isn't based on the size of your business or how many systems you service.

Will you work with the dispatch software I already use?

I don't replace the field service software your team already dispatches from — I plug into it. ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, FieldEdge, and more — tell us what you run at signup and we'll get it connected, so every booked job and contact lands right on the board your team already checks.

Is there a contract, or can I cancel anytime?

No contract. HelloBob is billed month to month at one flat rate, and you can cancel anytime.

Can you talk to Spanish-speaking customers too?

Yes — I can be configured to answer in English or Spanish. This site itself has an EN/ES toggle in the top navigation, so you can see exactly how that looks before you sign up.

Can I see you in action before I sign up?

Of course — check out the See It In Action tab above. You can click through real ways customers reach me: website chat, Google Business Profile, missed-call text-back, review follow-up, Facebook, Instagram, QR codes on your trucks, and more.

Is it even legal to text my customers like this?

Yes, when it's done right — and that's exactly what I handle. I only text customers who said yes, I keep reminders, on-my-way alerts, invoices, and review requests under one clear consent instead of asking five separate times, I respect quiet hours, and in states with extra texting rules (Florida, Oklahoma, Washington, Maryland, Connecticut, Texas, New York) I automatically apply the tighter limits those states require. You don't have to become a compliance expert to use me.
